ABOUT

I'm a Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Linköping University, specialising in Applied Mechanics with a focus on Computational Fluid Dynamics and Finite Element Analysis. My work spans the full simulation lifecycle — defining the physical problem, building and meshing the geometry, running and monitoring the solve, and verifying and validating the result against DNS, experiment, or analytical theory — rather than any single step in isolation.

Before starting my Master's, I worked as a Mechanical Engineer in the oil and gas sector, where I supported Qatar Energy as a client. That role involved Bow-Tie risk analysis, and developing Safety Critical Element (SCE) performance standards and human factors assessments — work that put a premium on rigour and defensible numbers, a habit that now carries directly into how I validate every simulation I run.

HOW I WORK

The full simulation lifecycle, not just the validation step

01

Define

Physics, assumptions, boundary conditions, and the engineering question the simulation actually needs to answer.

02

Geometry & Mesh

CAD cleanup, domain sizing, and a mesh strategy matched to the near-wall and flow-feature resolution required.

03

Solve

Solver and turbulence-model selection, convergence monitoring, and run-time diagnostics.

04

Verify & Validate

Mesh/time-step independence, and comparison against DNS, experiment, or closed-form theory.

05

Decide

Turning the validated result into an actual engineering recommendation.

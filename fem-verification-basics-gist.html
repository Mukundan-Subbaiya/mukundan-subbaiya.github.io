GIST & RESULT

FE Verification: Beam, Thick Pipe & Notched Bar

ANSYS MechanicalAnalytical BenchmarkingMesh Convergence

Three classic elasticity problems, each solved by FEM and checked line-for-line against a closed-form analytical solution — the discipline every later, more complex model in this portfolio is built on.

Setup

Three progressively more demanding problems were solved and checked against theory: an end-loaded cantilever beam (displacement, bending and shear stress), an infinite thick pipe under internal pressure (radial/hoop stress and displacement across 2D plane-stress, plane-strain, and axisymmetric idealizations), and a flat bar with edge notches under a stress concentration.

Validation

Every FEM result was plotted directly against its analytical counterpart, not just summarized as a single error number. For the notched bar, an automatic adaptive mesh-refinement study was run through six successive solutions to confirm the stress concentration had actually converged, rather than stopping at an arbitrary mesh density.

Result

The notch-root stress concentration converged from an initial 14.82 MPa to a stable 15.06 MPa — within 3.2% of the analytical handbook value for that geometry. The beam and thick-pipe cases showed similarly close agreement across displacement, stress, and the different 2D idealizations, confirming the FE setup, element choice, and boundary conditions before any of this methodology was extended to the more complex models elsewhere in this portfolio.
